# Customer Service FAQ — PawPilot (Pet Supplies)

Internal quick-reference answers for front-line customer service. Answers are written in English
because U.S. marketplace customers are the primary audience; Chinese staff can translate as needed.

## Q1: Is this toy safe for puppies?

A: Our cotton rope toys are designed for medium-to-large dogs and aggressive chewers. For
puppies under 6 months, we recommend the Small size only under supervision. Always remove the
toy if pieces begin to fray or break off. The product is not intended for children.

## Q2: What is your return policy?

A: We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for items in original condition. Refunds are
processed once the item is received at the fulfillment center. For defective or damaged items,
please contact us through your Amazon order page and we will arrange a replacement or full
refund without requiring a return.

## Q3: How do I choose the right harness size?

A: Measure your dog's chest girth at the widest part behind the front legs. Compare this
measurement to our size chart. If your dog is between sizes or has a thick coat, choose the
larger size. The harness should allow two fingers to slide under any strap.

## Q4: Can I machine wash the harness?

A: Yes, place the harness in a mesh laundry bag and wash on a gentle cycle with cold water. Air
dry only; do not tumble dry, as heat may warp the buckles. Do not use fabric softener.

## Q5: Is the rope toy indestructible?

A: No toy is indestructible. Our rope toys are made with durable cotton braid designed for
aggressive chewers, but they will wear over time. Inspect the toy after each play session and
replace it when fraying exposes the inner filler. Supervise play, especially with power chewers.

## Q6: What is the difference between the front and back leash clips?

A: The back clip is for everyday walks with a dog that does not pull. The front clip is a
no-pull training aid: when the dog pulls, the harness turns the dog toward you, reducing
forward momentum. It is a training tool, not an instant fix, and works best with consistent
handling.

## Q7: Do your products contain lead or AZO dyes?

A: No. Our webbing and rope materials are tested for lead content and AZO dyes by third-party
labs. Test reports are available on request through Seller Central messaging.

## Q8: Why does my package look different from the photo?

A: We occasionally update packaging to reduce waste or improve protection. The product inside is
the same. If you received the wrong item entirely, please contact us through your Amazon order
page with a photo and we will resolve it.
