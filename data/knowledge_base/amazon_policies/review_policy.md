# Amazon Customer Reviews Policy — Operational Reference

Source: Amazon Seller Central — Community Guidelines and Customer Reviews Policy. This document
covers what sellers may and may not do with customer reviews, and how to respond to negative
reviews.

## What Sellers Are Prohibited From Doing

- Never ask customers to change or remove a review in exchange for a refund, discount, free
  product, or any other benefit.
- Do not ask family members, employees, or friends to post reviews of your products or
  competitors' products.
- Do not offer compensation of any kind in exchange for a review, including through third-party
  services, websites, social media groups, or rebate clubs.
- Do not post reviews on your own products or your competitors' products.
- Do not use a customer review to disparage another seller or product.
- Do not manipulate the helpfulness voting on reviews.

## Requesting Reviews Legally

- You may use Amazon's "Request a Review" button in Seller Central (automated within 5–30 days
  of order delivery) or third-party tools that send only Amazon's pre-approved message.
- You may include a neutral product insert that asks for a review honestly, without conditioning
  it on a positive review and without offering an incentive.
- Best practice: include a clear, unconditional request such as "Your feedback helps other pet
  owners. Please consider leaving an honest review on Amazon."

## Review Removal

Amazon will remove reviews that violate its community guidelines, including:

- Reviews containing obscene or abusive language.
- Reviews that include personal information.
- Reviews that are entirely about fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) issues, not the product.
- Reviews from a confirmed competitor or someone with a financial interest.
- Reviews that appear fraudulent or incentivized.

To request removal, use Seller Central > Brands > Customer Reviews. Amazon decides; sellers
cannot directly delete reviews.

## Responding to Negative Reviews

- Keep replies short, factual, and solution-oriented.
- Acknowledge the specific concern and state the action you are taking or have taken.
- Do not argue, blame the customer, or use templated apologies that sound insincere.
- Example: "Thank you for your feedback. We're sorry the size did not fit your dog. We have
  updated our size chart and added a breed-weight guide in the images. Please contact our
  support team for a replacement or refund."
- Move refund or replacement requests off the public review page by directing the customer to
  Seller Central messaging.

## Using Reviews for Product Improvement

- Aggregate reviews by theme (quality, sizing, packaging, instructions, durability) before
  making design or listing changes.
- Track review velocity (reviews per unit sold) and star-trend over time. A sudden drop often
  indicates a manufacturing batch issue or listing mismatch.
- Update the listing copy or images to address the top complaint if it is recurring, then
  measure whether the same complaint volume declines in the next 30 days.
