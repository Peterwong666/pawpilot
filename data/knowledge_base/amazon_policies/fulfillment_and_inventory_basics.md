# Amazon Fulfillment and Inventory Basics — Operational Reference

Source: Amazon Seller Central — FBA, FBM, and inventory planning fundamentals. Use this document
to answer operations questions about fulfillment options, fees, and inventory management.

## Fulfillment Models

### FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon)

- Seller ships inventory to Amazon fulfillment centers.
- Amazon handles storage, picking, packing, shipping, customer service, and returns.
- Products become Prime-eligible, which increases Buy Box eligibility and conversion.
- Fees include fulfillment fees (per unit, based on size and weight) and storage fees (monthly
  and long-term).

### FBM (Fulfillment by Merchant)

- Seller stores inventory and ships orders directly to customers.
- Seller is responsible for meeting Amazon's shipping and delivery promises.
- Useful for oversized items, low-turnover SKUs, or products that require special handling.

### Seller Fulfilled Prime (SFP)

- Seller ships from own warehouse but offers Prime delivery promises.
- Requires meeting strict performance metrics and pre-approval from Amazon.
- Suitable for sellers with mature logistics operations.

## FBA Fee Components

- **Fulfillment fee**: charged per unit shipped, based on product size tier and shipping weight.
- **Storage fee**: charged monthly based on average daily volume (cubic feet). Rates increase in
  October–December (peak season).
- **Long-term storage fee**: charged for inventory stored more than 365 days.
- **Removal / disposal fee**: charged when inventory is removed from fulfillment centers.
- **Returns processing fee**: charged for select categories when a customer returns a product.

## Inventory Management Basics

- **Inventory Performance Index (IPI)**: score from 0 to 1,000 that measures how efficiently you
  manage FBA inventory. A score below the threshold (historically around 400–500) may result in
  storage limits.
- **Sell-through rate**: units sold / average units in stock over 90 days. Higher is better.
- **Excess inventory**: inventory with more than 90 days of supply based on forecast demand.
- **Stranded inventory**: inventory in FBA that is not active on a listing, generating storage
  fees without sales potential.

## Best Practices for Pet Supplies

- Avoid sending large quantities of seasonal items too early; pet products with strong seasonality
  (cooling mats in summer, holiday costumes) can incur long-term storage fees if demand is
  overestimated.
- Bundle slow movers with fast movers to improve sell-through where it makes brand sense.
- Monitor size-tier changes. A small packaging reduction can drop a product to a lower size tier,
  significantly reducing fulfillment fees.
- Keep a 30–60 day supply of top SKUs to balance stockout risk and storage cost.

## Key Reports to Monitor

- **Inventory Ledger**: daily inventory movement (received, sold, returned, removals).
- **Manage FBA Inventory**: stranded inventory, IPI contributors, recommended actions.
- **Restock Inventory**: demand forecast and recommended shipments.
- **Fee Preview**: estimated fulfillment and storage fees per ASIN.
