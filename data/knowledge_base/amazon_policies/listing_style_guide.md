# Amazon Listing Style Guide — Key Requirements

Source: Amazon Seller Central listing style guidelines (pets category). Applies to all new and
updated listings in the Pet Supplies category.

## Title Requirements

The title is the single highest-weighted field for search indexing. Follow these rules:

- Maximum length is 200 characters in the Pet Supplies category. Titles longer than 200
  characters will be suppressed or blocked at submission.
- Recommended structure: `Brand + Product Type + Key Attribute + Size/Quantity + Color`.
  Example: "PawPilot Cotton Rope Dog Toy for Aggressive Chewers, Large, 3-Pack".
- Use capitalization for the first letter of each word except prepositions, conjunctions,
  and articles shorter than five letters (in, on, of, the, and, for, with).
- Do not use ALL CAPS for the entire title. All-caps titles are auto-flagged by listing quality
  checks and degrade conversion.
- Do not include promotional phrases anywhere in the title: "Free Shipping", "100% Quality
  Guaranteed", "Hot Sale", "Best Seller", "Limited Time Offer".
- Do not include subjective claims or ranking claims: "Best", "Top Rated", "#1", "Cheapest",
  "Most Popular".
- Do not use non-standard symbols in the title: `! $ ? _ ~ { } ^ ¬ ¦`. Hyphens, commas,
  periods, and forward slashes are allowed.
- Do not include your seller name, phone number, email, website, or price in the title.
- Spell out units of measure: "3-Pack" rather than "3PK", "24 Ounces" rather than "24oz" is
  preferred for readability.

## Bullet Points (Key Product Features)

- Maximum 500 characters per bullet; recommended 150–250 characters for readability on mobile.
- Use sentence fragments; do not end bullets with a full stop.
- Begin each bullet with a capital letter.
- The first bullet should state the primary benefit, not the primary feature. "Keeps
  aggressive chewers engaged for hours" outperforms "Made of cotton rope".
- Include a size/dimension bullet for any product with physical sizing (pet apparel, beds,
  collars, harnesses). Missing size bullets are a top driver of returns in pet apparel.
- Include care and material information when relevant: "Machine washable, air dry".
- Do not repeat the title verbatim in a bullet; the duplicated text adds no search value.
- Place warranty or guarantee statements in the final bullet only, and phrase them factually:
  "Backed by a 12-month manufacturer warranty" rather than "100% satisfaction guaranteed".

## Product Description and A+ Content

- Description limit is 2,000 characters when A+ Content is not used.
- Do not include seller contact details, external URLs, or promotional language such as
  "cheapest on Amazon" — these are policy violations subject to listing suppression.
- A+ Content (brand-registered sellers): use comparison charts to show size options; modules
  with lifestyle imagery outperform plain text blocks by a wide margin in Pet Supplies.

## Image Requirements

- Main image must have a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255) and show the actual product,
  not packaging or accessories not included in the offer.
- The product must fill at least 85% of the main image frame.
- Minimum 1000 pixels on the longest side to enable zoom; 1600 pixels or larger is recommended.
- No graphics, logos, watermarks, or inset images on the main image.
- Additional images should include: scale reference (product next to a common object or a
  pet of known size), material close-up, in-use lifestyle photos, and packaging contents.
- Text overlays are allowed on secondary images only, and must not contain promotional claims
  or the word "free".

## Category-Specific Notes for Pet Supplies

- Pet apparel must include a size chart image and a breed-weight reference in the bullets.
- Pet food and treats listings must display guaranteed analysis and calorie content in the
  images or A+ Content; see the pet food compliance guide for labeling rules.
- Electronic pet products (water fountains, GPS trackers, smart feeders) must show the plug
  type and voltage in images and state certifications (UL, ETL, FCC) in the bullets.
