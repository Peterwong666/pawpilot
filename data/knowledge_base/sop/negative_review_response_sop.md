# Negative Review Response SOP — PawPilot Customer Service

## Purpose

Respond to negative reviews quickly, professionally, and in a way that protects account health
while converting unhappy customers into retained buyers.

## Response Window

- Check new reviews daily.
- Respond to 1–3 star reviews within 24 business hours.
- Do not respond to reviews that contain profanity or threats until after escalation.

## Response Template (Adjust for Specific Issue)

1. Thank the customer for the feedback.
2. Apologize briefly and specifically for the issue described.
3. State the corrective action already taken or offered.
4. Direct the customer to Seller Central messaging for refund/replacement/logistics.
5. Keep the reply under 200 words.

## Example Responses by Issue Type

### Sizing Issue

"Thank you for your honest review. We're sorry the harness did not fit your dog as expected.
Our size chart uses chest girth at the widest point behind the front legs; if your measurement
fell near the upper end of a size, choosing the next size up usually resolves the fit. Please
contact our support team through your Amazon order page and we will arrange an exchange or
refund."

### Durability / Damage Issue

"Thank you for letting us know. We design our rope toys for aggressive chewers, but no toy is
completely indestructible. Please supervise play and remove the toy once fraying exposes the
inner filler. We'd like to learn more about how the damage occurred so we can improve the
product. Please message us through your order page for a replacement."

### Shipping / Packaging Issue

"Thank you for your feedback. We are sorry the package arrived damaged. Fulfillment issues are
handled by Amazon's FBA team, and we have opened a case to address the packing quality. Please
contact us through your order page so we can make this right."

### Listing Description Mismatch

"Thank you for pointing this out. We want the listing to match what you receive. We have noted
your concern and will review the images and description for clarity. Please reach out through
your order page so we can resolve this for you."

## What Not to Do

- Do not ask the customer to change or remove the review.
- Do not offer compensation publicly in the review reply.
- Do not blame the customer or argue about the facts.
- Do not use the same copied reply for every review; personalize at least one sentence.

## Logging

After responding, log the review date, star rating, issue theme, response sent, and any follow-up
action in the weekly review summary sheet. Use the theme data to prioritize product and listing
improvements.
