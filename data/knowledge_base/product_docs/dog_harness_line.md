## Product Overview

An adjustable no-pull dog harness with front and back leash attachments. Designed for daily walks
and training for dogs that pull on leash.

## Materials

- Outer layer: Oxford nylon 1680D (water-resistant, abrasion-resistant).
- Padding: Breathable air-mesh on chest and belly panels.
- Buckles: Duraflex side-release buckles.
- Leash attachments: Zinc alloy D-rings (front and back).
- Reflective stitching on chest strap and back strap for low-light visibility.

## Certifications

- AZO-free and lead-free test report on webbing.
- Duraflex buckle load test: 150 kg static pull per buckle.

## Size and Fit Guidance

- Measure the chest girth at the widest part behind the front legs. This is the decisive
  measurement; neck girth is secondary.
- If the measurement is near the upper limit of a size, choose the next size up to allow for
  coat thickness and weight fluctuation.
- The harness should allow two fingers to slide under any strap.

## Care Instructions

- Hand wash or machine wash in a mesh bag on gentle cycle.
- Air dry; do not tumble dry (may warp buckles).
- Check buckle function after each wash.

## Listing Notes (Internal)

- Title target: "PawPilot No Pull Dog Harness with Front Clip, Reflective, Adjustable, [Size], [Color]".
- Avoid "guaranteed to stop pulling" — this is a training aid claim and can be challenged.
  Approved phrasing: "front clip design helps reduce pulling during walks".
- Required images: size chart (chest + neck ranges), reflective detail close-up, dog in harness
  (lifestyle), buckle close-up.
- Top negative review themes: sizing runs small, strap rubs on short-haired dogs, front clip
  twists when dog spins.
- Recommended response SOP: apologize, ask for chest measurement, offer exchange or refund, and
  log the case for design review.
