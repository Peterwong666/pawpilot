## Product Overview

A line of cotton rope chew toys for medium-to-large dogs and aggressive chewers. The product is
positioned as a durable, washable alternative to rubber and nylon toys.

## Materials

- Outer braid: 100% natural cotton (undyed base yarn).
- Inner filler: Food-grade cotton stuffing, no synthetic fibers.
- Dye: Pet-safe, AZO-free fabric dye used only on accent threads.
- No glue, no metal, no squeaker.

## Certifications

- AZO-free dye certificate from supplier.
- Third-party lab test for small parts (16 CFR Part 1501 simulated-use test) — PASS for the
  product as a pet toy, with adult supervision.
- Material safety data sheet available on request.

## Care Instructions

- Machine washable in cold water on gentle cycle.
- Air dry; do not tumble dry (shrinkage risk).
- Inspect after each play session; remove and replace if fraying exposes inner filler.
