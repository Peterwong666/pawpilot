## Product Overview

A slow feeder bowl designed to reduce eating speed in dogs, reducing the risk of bloat and
indigestion. Made from food-safe, BPA-free material.

## Materials

- Body: Food-grade PP (polypropylene), BPA-free, phthalate-free.
- Surface: Textured maze pattern with smooth rounded edges.
- Non-slip base: TPR rubber ring.
- Dishwasher safe (top rack).

## Certifications

- FDA-compliant food-contact material certificate.
- BPA-free test report.

## Care Instructions

- Dishwasher safe on top rack, or hand wash with warm soapy water.
- Do not microwave.
- Inspect for cracks every 2–3 weeks; replace if damaged.

## Customer Service Notes

- If a customer reports the bowl slides, ask them to confirm the rubber ring is fully seated and
  the floor surface is dry.
- If the maze is hard to clean, recommend soaking in warm water before dishwasher cycle.
